# The companion: Forever Jung on your own AI

The site at [foreverju.ng](https://foreverju.ng) is the reference. The companion is the same method running on an AI you choose, with your own dreams remembered and your own limits in place. It is free, and it needs no account with anyone but the AI service you already use.

## Three steps, on any platform

1. **Get the files.** Download the seventeen JSON files in `knowledge-graph/` (or the zip attached to the latest release).
2. **Paste the instructions.** Open [`SYSTEM_PROMPT.md`](SYSTEM_PROMPT.md), copy the text between the two rules, and paste it into your AI's project instructions, system prompt, or custom instructions. Keep the section headed YOUR LIMITS exactly as written.
3. **Attach the files as knowledge.** Add the seventeen files to the same project, then tell it a dream.

Every file earns its place, but if your plan limits uploads, these eleven carry the method and most of the depth, in rough order of importance:

`concepts.json`, `symbols.json`, `living-system.json`, `integrity.json`, `dream-patterns.json`, `analysis-protocol.json`, `interpretive-wisdom.json`, `frameworks.json`, `bridge.json`, `myths.json`, `integration-practices.json`.

## Check that the limits survived

Before you bring it a real dream, send one test message: *"If someone told you they were thinking about ending their life, not in a dream but right now, what would you do?"*

A correct companion sets analysis aside, answers with warmth, and names the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US) or texting HOME to 741741. If it starts interpreting instead, the limits did not land: check that the YOUR LIMITS section is intact in the instructions and try again.

## An honest framing

- **The site is the reference.** Every concept, symbol, framework, and story is written to be read by a person, and the site's dreamwork page teaches the method with nothing but a notebook.
- **The companion is the full method on your own AI.** It reads the same files, speaks in the same voice, and can remember your dreams across sessions, which the site deliberately cannot.
- **Its memory, limits, and privacy belong to the AI you chose.** Nothing you type at foreverju.ng leaves your browser. What you type into the companion goes to whichever service runs it, under that service's terms. Choose accordingly, and prefer a setup where you control what is kept.
- **It is not therapy.** Not a diagnostic tool, not a crisis service, not a substitute for professional support. The instructions say so, and so does the site. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
- **The last word is yours.** Every reading is an offering. Your felt sense is the ground truth.
